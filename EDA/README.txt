E-Commerce Product Demand Dataset

A synthetic e-commerce dataset created for product demand analysis,
demand forecasting, regression, machine learning, and business analytics.

Rows: 8,000
Columns: 25
Date range: 2022-01-01 to 2025-12-31
Primary target: Demand_Units

The dataset includes:
- Product and category information
- Date, season, region, and sales channel
- Product price and competitor price
- Discounts and promotions
- Marketing spend and search interest
- Website traffic
- Stock availability
- Customer ratings and return rates
- Holiday and new-product indicators
- Delivery time
- Demand in units
- Estimated revenue
- Inventory turnover index

Suggested use cases:
- Product demand forecasting
- E-commerce sales prediction
- Inventory planning
- Demand regression
- Product performance analysis
- Pricing and promotion analysis
- Customer and market analytics
- Machine learning projects

Data provenance:
This is a fully synthetic dataset generated with Python using statistical
distributions and controlled business relationships. It contains no real
customer, company, or personally identifiable information.
